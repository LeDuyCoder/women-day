# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A woman receiving a personal October 20 greeting from a friend, and the person preparing the digital gift.

## Product Purpose

A one-page Vietnamese Women's Day greeting that lets the recipient open a sincere letter, browse playful gift ideas, and discover a locked surprise. Success means the page feels personal, romantic, easy to use, and delightful on a phone.

## Brand Personality

Romantic, sweet, modern, and lightly playful. The writing should feel warm and sincere, with gentle teasing reserved for the locked gift.

## Anti-references

No specific reference sites were provided. Avoid clutter, childish candy-pink styling, and generic template-like decoration.

## Design Principles

- Let the greeting and letter carry the emotional weight.
- Make the envelope interaction understandable and satisfying.
- Keep the gift joke affectionate and easy to dismiss.
- Keep the experience comfortable to read and use on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special accessibility requirement was specified. Use readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, semantic content, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
